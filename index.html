Quick answers

FTL or PTL?

FTL is a whole dedicated reefer. PTL shares a truck with compatible cargo and you pay for your weight, volume or pallets.

Can two temperatures share a truck?

Yes, on multi-temperature vehicles with insulated bulkheads. Each compartment holds its own band.

Do you carry pharma?

Only after licensing, packaging and quality validation, and always in a dedicated compartment or vehicle.

How do I know it stayed cold?

A live temperature graph during the trip and a recorded handover temperature at delivery.
